## 1. What is Domain Authority?

Domain authority (DA) is a score of search engine rank that provides metrics of overall site performance on SERPs. DA is created by Moz, a software development company to check and predict how your website will likely perform on SERP.

The quality and quantity of your backlinks determine your domain authority, which ultimately affects your Google ranking. So, there’s a correlation between domain authority and ranking. Higher DA → higher ranking; lower DA → lower ranking.

The score lies between 1 and 100 points to predict how well the site can rank in search engines. The higher the number of points, the better the performance; the lower the number of points, the worse the performance. A website’s strength is indicated by its score. The strength is in the number of links and the authority of those links.

## 2. Why is Domain Authority Important?

“85% of SEOs and marketers believe that link building has a big impact on brand authority and brand building.”

Domain authority is a mirror for your site’s performance. It is not a direct ranking factor, but it indirectly pushes your site to rank higher.

When determining a website’s ranking on a search engine page, Google considers its authority and relevance.

How does that happen?

Backlinks. Yes, the links related to high-performing sites are credible. Plus, Google finds it authentic. Hence, more authentic backlinks will increase your site’s chances to rank higher on search engine result pages.

The higher the DA, the better the chances are to rank on the SERP.

Higher search engine ranking correlates with higher domain authority. But it does not impact the ranking of the site.

The quality and quantity of the links have an impact on the website.

The higher the domain rating, the more difficult it is to increase the rating. The reason is the logarithmic scale. That is why for websites with high DA scores like 80 or 90 it is hard to score more.

For instance, scoring a DR of 75 to 80 is more difficult than scoring 20 to 30.

Keep in mind, the DA score is not a metric that Google uses to rank websites on SERP.

## 6. Are Domain Authority And Page Authority Same?

Many people have this question. Although DA and PA look alike, there is a slight line between them to differentiate. As we all know,

### What is Page Authority?

Page authority is also a metric produced by Moz to predict how a page is likely to perform on a search engine result page. Like DA, PA is calculated on a logarithmic scale ranging from 0 to 100. The higher you score, the higher your page rank on SEP.

## 7. How Are Domain Authority and Page Authority Different?

Page authority focuses on specific page metrics to rank on the SERP. While domain authority focuses on all website domains as a whole for ranking on SERP.

In a nutshell, DA takes into account the overall ranking strength of domains and subdomains, whereas PA takes into account the individual ranking strength of individual pages.

### (2) How Does Moz Determine Domain Authority Score?

Moz calculates domain authority with multiple factors, including linking root domains and the total number of links, considering a single DA score. It’s a comparison of websites or gauging the “ranking strength” of a website over time.

For better understanding, check out this: Moz Domain Authority Guide.

**Moz has confirmed some factors for DA calculations:**

- Inbound links- the number of inbound links referring to a website from different domains.
- Quality of links- the authority of the link and the website linking to a domain.
- Root domains- Moz counts the unique backlinks coming from unique sites. However, Moz considers 100 sites linked from a single site to be one root domain.
- Content quality- The quality of the content and domain rankings on the Google search engine result page.
- On-page SEO- The on-page SEO score of the website.

Increasing domain authority is a daunting process, but if you choose the right strategy and keep moving you’re not far away from seeing results. Here are some simple and easy tips you can implement. You might have this question.

**Do the Following First To Increase Domain Authority:**

### 1) Create Link Worthy Killer Content:

First things first, you need high-quality content. Mediocre content won’t do well at getting high-quality links. Hardly anyone wants to get links from such content. So create content that is link-worthy.

According to Moz, blog posts with 3000+ words receive more backlinks than blog posts under 3000 words. Such content provides in-depth information about the topic and value to the audience.

A study found, good SEO-friendly content rich in targeted keywords will rank you higher organically. Furthermore, content with original data and research is most effective to earn backlinks.

As you can be easily found on search engine result pages, your chances of getting backlinks increase.

You’ll get natural backlinks when you have optimized content.

That shows that you’ve got the potential to rank higher because of your strong domain score.

Creating high-quality content is a requirement to increase your DA score.

### 2) Link Audit

Link auditing is the process of getting and analyzing backlinks pointing to your website to find good and bad backlinks. You’ll know all spammy backlinks that negatively impact your ranking. A link audit is significant if your website is 3–4 months old. Examine the domains that are linked. Remove spammy links with a 30% spam score.

Where can I find backlinks to your website?

- Google Search Console
- Ahrefs
- Semrush
- Moz
- Majestic
- Bing Webmaster Tool

With the help of these tools, find the linking domains of your website.

- Check the spam score of these domains linking to your site.
- Compare your referring domains to those of your competitors.
- A constant increase in backlinks over a period of time indicates the growth of your domain, which is good.
- Check your anchor text distribution.
- In backlink reports, first, start with a web page, not an entire domain.

If you have more website links with high DA scores, then a few spammy backlinks won’t affect your DA score.

But it is necessary to remove spammy links that in the near future might hurt the DA score of your site as well as its SERP ranking.

#### Collect the Data and Filter These:

**Dead links-** The sites that return 403, 404, 410, 503, etc. responses are dead links. Spammy links removal is the basic thing to do for increasing domain authority.

**Nofollow links –** Nofollow links are not harmful unless they exceed the limit. In fact they do the opposite. According to a recent study, Google will decide whether to pass or not to pass ranking credit for nofollow links and whether to discard or not whenever the nofollow attribute is added. This makes nofollow links more valuable than ever before.

The main reason behind the reduction of your website domain rating is high rates of spammy domains. Spammy backlinks are rooted in poor-quality websites of lower DA score that negatively impact your domain authority.

### 9) Get Links from Authoritative and Relevant Websites:

To increase domain authority, build links from high-authority sites. A poor-quality link will harm your DA score. Again, choose quality over quantity. With a planned and effective link-building strategy, it’s easy for you to improve your DA score in less time.

- Google Analytics will also help you track the broken links.
- Create better, updated, and value-driven content.
- Reach out to site owners by contact form or email and offer the current URL to link your site. They’ll happily update their page with an updated link.

Rebuilding broken links is one of the best ways to boost the DA of your site.
